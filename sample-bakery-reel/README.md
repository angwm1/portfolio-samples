# Sample Bakery promo reel

A 17-second vertical video (1080 x 1920, 30 fps) for a fictional bakery. It shows the kind of short promo a small food business can post on Instagram Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

- **Footage:** two 8-second clips made with an AI video model (Gemini). The end card says so.
- **Edit:** the hook lines, price cards, crossfades and end card are built in code with HyperFrames (an open-source HTML-to-video tool), so a new reel with a client's own photos, prices and opening hours is quick to produce.
- **Made up:** the business, prices, address and phone number. The number is a placeholder.

Files: `sample-bakery-reel.mp4` (web copy, H.264 + AAC, about 6 MB) and `poster.jpg` (the still shown before play).

For a real client the product shots would be the client's own photos or clips. AI footage suits mood shots only.

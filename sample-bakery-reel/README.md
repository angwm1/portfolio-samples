# Sample Bakery promo reel

A 17-second vertical video (1080 x 1920, 30 fps) for a fictional bakery. It shows the kind of short promo a small food business can post on Instagram Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

- **Footage:** three stock clips from Pexels (free to use under the Pexels licence; video IDs 30627114, 36825699 and 28739315). The end card says "stock footage".
- **Sound:** free sound effects from Pixabay (Pixabay Content License). Each knife sound is cut from a real recording and placed on a saw stroke that was measured in the clip, frame by frame, so the sound follows the picture. Each clip has its own sound bed on the timeline.
- **Edit:** the hook lines, price cards, crossfades and end card are built in code with HyperFrames (an open-source HTML-to-video tool), so a new reel with a client's own photos, prices and opening hours is quick to produce.
- **Made up:** the business, prices, address and phone number. The number is a placeholder.

Files: `sample-bakery-reel.mp4` (web copy, H.264 + AAC, about 3 MB) and `poster.jpg` (the still shown before play).

For a real client the product shots would be the client's own photos or clips; the stock footage here only stands in for them.
